About TutorSetu

Find the Right Tutor. Learn the Right Way.

TutorSetu is a tutor marketplace built for West Bengal. We connect students and parents in Howrah, Kolkata and nearby areas with trusted local and online tutors.

"Setu" means bridge — and that's exactly what we are: a secure, transparent bridge between learners and educators.
